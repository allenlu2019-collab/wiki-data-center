Based on the latest industry information and Nvidia's roadmap as of mid-2026, the cooling technologies for Nvidia GPUs have evolved from traditional air cooling to advanced liquid-to-chip architectures. [[1](https://english.cw.com.tw/article/article.action?id=4503), [2](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/cop29-energy-efficiency-panel/)]

**1. Vera Rubin Generation (Latest / 2026+)**

- **Primary Technology:** **100% Liquid Cooling with "Warm Water" Architecture**
    - **Mechanism:** The Vera Rubin platform (specifically the NVL72 and NVL576 rack systems) utilizes a fully liquid-cooled infrastructure that circulates coolant at a high inlet temperature of **45°C (113°F)**.
    - **Key Innovation:** By running at these higher temperatures, the system can use outdoor **dry coolers** (radiators) rather than energy-intensive mechanical chillers (refrigeration) in most climates. The coolant absorbs heat, exiting the system at approximately 65°C (149°F).
    - **Coolant Composition:** A mixture of **75% water and 25% propylene glycol** flows through closed loops directly to cold plates on the chips.
    - **Materials:** Some Rubin GPUs utilize a **diamond composite cooling solution** to improve thermal conductivity, and higher-end configurations (like Rubin Ultra) are beginning to adopt **Microchannel Lids (MCL)**. [[1]

**2. Rosa Feynman Generation (Future / ~2028-2030)**

- **Primary Technology:** **Microchannel Lids (MCL) & Direct-to-Silicon Cooling**
    - **The Challenge:** With projected power consumption reaching **5,000W–7,000W+** per package, traditional cold plates are insufficient.
    - **The Solution:** The Feynman generation is expected to standardize **Microchannel Lids (MCL)**. This technology integrates cooling channels directly into the chip's protective lid, eliminating the thermal paste layer (TIM2) that typically acts as a heat bottleneck.
    - **System Level:** Feynman systems will likely rely extensively on **Co-Packaged Optics (CPO)** to reduce the heat generated by electrical data transmission, further managing the thermal load. 

**3. Blackwell Generation (Predecessor / 2024-2025)**
- **Primary Technology:** **Hybrid Air & Liquid Cooling**
    - **Transition Era:** Blackwell marked the shift from air to liquid. While standard configurations (like the HGX B200) could still use air cooling, high-density versions (like the **GB200 NVL72**) required liquid cooling.
    - **Tech Specs:** Utilizes **Direct-to-Chip (D2C)** cold plates with inlet temperatures typically lower than Rubin's 45°C standard. It focuses on reducing the need for fans but still often requires facility chillers depending on the deployment. [[1]

**4. Hopper Generation (Older / 2022-2023)**

- **Primary Technology:** **Air Cooling (Standard)**
    - **Mechanism:** The H100 and H200 series primarily rely on large heatsinks and high-speed fans (air cooling) for standard enterprise servers.
    - **Liquid Option:** Liquid cooling (cold plates) was available as an optional upgrade for specialized high-density clusters but was not the mandatory baseline architecture as it is for Rubin.

## 一、 Rubin Ultra 的晶片與機櫃功耗（Power Consumption）
AI 晶片每代功耗呈現指數級跳躍（Hopper 700W → Blackwell 1,000W-1,400W → Rubin 1,800W-2,300W）。到了 Rubin Ultra 世代，數字再度被改寫： [3, 4, 5] 

* 單顆晶片（GPU Package）功耗： 最高可達 3,600W (熱設計功耗 TDP)。
* 註： 雖然供應鏈在 2026 上半年傳出由於 TSMC 的 CoWoS-L 先進封裝良率與延遲考量，Nvidia 將原本設計的「4 顆運算晶粒（Compute Die）+ 16 顆 HBM4e」暫時修改為雙晶粒架構，但其最大極限 Max-P 功耗在完整解鎖狀態下依然會鎖定在 3.6 kW 級別。這意味著單顆 GPU 消耗的電量就足以驅動一台大型家用冷氣機。 [6, 7, 8] 
* 旗艦級「Kyber」機櫃系統（NVL576）總功耗： 高達 600 kW（千瓦）！
* 對比 Blackwell NVL72 的 120kW 機櫃，Rubin Ultra NVL576（Kyber）機櫃將一舉灌入高達 576 顆 GPU（透過 144 個晶片封裝組成），整台機櫃所需的電量飆升至 600 kW（近 0.6 兆瓦）。這導致傳統的 AC 交流電供電鏈無法負荷，逼使資料中心全面導入 800V 高壓直流（800V DC）電源專屬側櫃。 [3, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12] 

------------------------------
## 二、 Rubin Ultra 的散熱解決方案（Cooling Solution）
面對單顆 3,600W 的恐怖熱源，傳統水冷板（Cold Plate）的導熱效率與熱阻已達物理極限。Nvidia 為 Rubin Ultra 量身打造了以下三大液冷核心技術： [2, 8] 
## 1. 微通道蓋板（MLCP / MCL）的全面加速導入

* 技術落地： 雖然標準版 Rubin（2,300W）部分可延用既有 Oberon 機櫃的傳統直接晶片液冷（D2C）進行微幅優化，但 Rubin Ultra 的 3,600W 則強制綁定微通道蓋板（MCL）技術。
* 機制： 冷卻水路不透過外部設備，而是直接由奇鋐（AVC）、雙鴻、或是亞洲 vital（AVC）等大廠與封裝廠合作，直接在晶片金屬蓋內部蝕刻出微米級流道，以最微觀的「超短路徑」直接將 HBM4e 與 GPU 核心的局部極熱點（Hotspots）快速降溫。其單價高達傳統水冷板的 3 至 7 倍。 [2, 7, 13, 14, 15] 

## 2. 立體高壓分流結構（3D Micro-fluidic Manifold）

* 為了防止 3,600W 晶片局部沸騰（Boil-off）或產生熱飽和，MCL 上方整合了極複雜的 In-Lid Manifold（內建分流歧管）。冷水從頂部進入後，不是沿著晶片表面單向流動，而是透過 3D 結構垂直且均勻地噴淋（Jet-channel Injection）至晶片最燙的核心正上方，再立刻從兩側廢水管吸走，藉此消除任何因水流速度不均產生的散熱盲區。 [8, 14, 16] 

## 3. 完全無風扇（Fanless）與 45°C 暖水冷卻架構 [17, 18] 

* 完全零氣冷： 在 Kyber（NVL576）機櫃內部，運算層與 NVLink 交換器層完全移除了風扇，實現 100% 純液冷運行。
* CDU 與冷卻基礎設施升級： 水冷主系統循環流量比 Blackwell 世代直接翻倍。其進水溫度維持在 45°C 的「暖水」標準，離開機櫃時廢水達到 65°C 以上。這確保了在 600 kW 的極端熱耗下，資料中心依然能完全仰賴外部的室外乾冷器（Dry Coolers / Radiators）散熱，而不需要動用冰水主機（Chillers），維持極低的冷卻能耗比（PUE < 1.10）。 [10, 11, 12, 19, 20]

## MCL signify a engineering effort from external cooling provider to packing house and even foundry

Yes, Microchannel Lid (MCL) technology forces a massive integration shift, dragging the engineering effort backward from external system-level cooling providers directly into the packaging house and foundry. [1, 2]

With AI processor clusters pushing past 3,000W to 5,000W+ per package, cooling can no longer be a secondary system bolted onto a finished chip. It must be co-designed as a fundamental component of advanced semiconductor packaging. [1, 2, 3, 4]

The engineering efforts and boundaries shift drastically across the supply chain in this new landscape:

## 1. Shift away from External Cooling Providers (System Level)

- The Loss of Control: Historically, cooling companies like [Auras Technology](https://english.cw.com.tw/article/article.action?id=4503) or AVC designed system-level cold plates that were attached to the chip via thermal grease (TIM2) during final server assembly. [2, 5]
- System to Component: With MCL, the traditional "cold plate" is eliminated entirely. The lid _is_ the cold plate. External providers are pushed out of the immediate chip interface and are instead relegated to managing the macro-plumbing, such as quick-disconnect valves, manifolds, and Coolant Distribution Units (CDUs) at the rack level. [1, 3, 6]

## 2. Aggressive Entry of Specialist Component / Lid Makers

- Precision Engineering: Specialist component makers with extensive histories in Vapor Chambers (VC) and structural chip stiffeners—most notably Taiwan's [Jentech Precision Industrial](https://www.jentech.com.tw/zh/vapor-chamber-lid-2)—have become the primary vanguard for MCL. [1, 7]
- The Semiconductor Standard: Because the lid directly encapsulates the multi-die architecture (GPU + HBM), these suppliers must achieve micrometer-level precision to match the warpage characteristics of the substrate. They have had to elevate their manufacturing lines to cleanroom, semiconductor-grade environments to prevent any particle contamination or metal micro-debris that could block sub-100μm channels. [1, 6]

## 3. Heavy Integration with Packaging Houses (OSATs)

- The Failure Boundary: In traditional systems, if a liquid cold plate leaks, you swap out the server module. With MCL, the lid is glued and sealed directly onto the substrate at the packaging plant. If an MCL clogs or develops a micro-fissure, a multi-thousand-dollar AI chip is ruined instantly. [1, 2]
- OSAT Responsibility: Packaging houses (like ASE or Amkor) must now extensively collaborate with component suppliers. They are forced to design new automated placement algorithms, structural testing methodologies, and pressure-sealing mechanics during the final assembly phases to guarantee a zero-leak, hermetic seal before the package ever ships to an ODM. [2, 8]

## 4. Ultimate Merging into the Foundry (e.g., TSMC)

- Silicon-Level Co-Design: For cutting-edge applications (like Nvidia's roadmaps), the foundry is the ultimate owner of thermal limits. The foundry dictates the exact layout of the thermal hot spots on the silicon die. [3]
- Direct-to-Silicon Evolution: Foundries like [TSMC](https://markets.financialcontent.com/stocks/article/tokenring-2026-1-15-the-end-of-air-cooling-tsmc-and-nvidia-pivot-to-direct-to-silicon-microfluidics-for-2000w-ai-superchips) have been co-developing microfluidics directly integrated into their advanced packaging pipelines (like CoWoS—Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate). Instead of just treating the lid as a piece of copper metal, the foundry provides thermal simulation profiles that tell the lid manufacturer exactly where the high-velocity micro-grooves must be etched to match the logic and memory layouts. [1, 2, 3]

## Summary of the New Supply Chain Hierarchy

MCL has effectively erased the line between "electronics packaging" and "plumbing." The engineering ownership has shifted from an aftermarket mechanical consideration to an upstream foundry and advanced packaging requirement, forcing structural lid manufacturers to behave like semiconductor component suppliers. [1, 2]

## TIM :**Thermal Interface Material**

It is a substance applied between a heat-generating component (like a CPU or GPU) and a cooling device (like a heat sink). Its purpose is to fill microscopic air gaps, vastly improving **thermal conductivity** and preventing the device from overheating. [[1](https://prod-edam.honeywell.com/content/dam/honeywell-edam/pmt/oneam/en-us/electronic-materials/thermal-interface-materials/documents/hon-ess-adm-the-role-of-tims-in-modern-electronics.pdf)]

Why TIM is Necessary

Air is a very poor conductor of heat. Even when two smooth metal surfaces (like a silicon chip and a copper heat sink) are pressed together, microscopic surface imperfections leave air gaps that trap heat. A TIM displaces this insulating air with a material that conducts heat much more efficiently, bridging the gap between the two surfaces. [[1](https://prod-edam.honeywell.com/content/dam/honeywell-edam/pmt/oneam/en-us/electronic-materials/thermal-interface-materials/documents/hon-ess-adm-the-role-of-tims-in-modern-electronics.pdf)]

Common Types of TIM

Understanding which material to use depends on the application, hardware type, and mounting pressure: [[1](https://resources.l-p.com/glossary/what-is-tim-thermal-interface-material)]

- **Thermal Paste (Thermal Grease):** The most common TIM. It is a highly viscous fluid that fills tiny crevices and provides excellent thermal conductivity. It requires mechanical pressure to keep the components tightly clamped. [[1](https://resources.l-p.com/glossary/what-is-tim-thermal-interface-material), [2](https://www.hala-tec.com/thermal-interface-materials/), [3](https://resources.l-p.com/de/glossary/what-is-tim-thermal-interface-material/), [4](https://cfe.com.tw/5-power-choke-inductor/tim-thermal-interface-material.pdf)]
- **Thermal Pads:** Pre-cut, soft, solid squares (typically made of silicone or paraffin). They are very easy to install and ideal for components of varying heights, though they conduct heat less effectively than paste. [[1](https://resources.l-p.com/glossary/what-is-tim-thermal-interface-material), [2](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Hrow87ukGNE)]
- **Liquid Metal:** Made from metal alloys that remain liquid at room temperature. They provide the absolute highest thermal conductivity, but require careful handling to prevent electrical shorts and corrosion. [[1](https://resources.l-p.com/glossary/what-is-tim-thermal-interface-material), [2](https://tark-solutions.com/Performance-Measurement-Thermoelectric-Modules), [3](https://www.trumonytechs.com/liquid-thermal-compound/), [4](https://cfe.com.tw/5-power-choke-inductor/tim-thermal-interface-material.pdf)]
- **Phase-Change Materials (PCMs):** Solid at room temperature but melt or soften when the device heats up, allowing them to perfectly conform to the interface. [[1](https://resources.l-p.com/glossary/what-is-tim-thermal-interface-material)]
- **Thermal Adhesives:** Similar to thermal paste, but they cure and harden to permanently bond the heat sink to the component. [[1](https://resources.l-p.com/glossary/what-is-tim-thermal-interface-material)]

How TIM is Categorized

In manufacturing, TIMs are sometimes divided by location and use-case: [[1](https://prod-edam.honeywell.com/content/dam/honeywell-edam/pmt/oneam/en-us/electronic-materials/thermal-interface-materials/documents/hon-ess-adm-the-role-of-tims-in-modern-electronics.pdf)]

- **TIM 1:** Materials applied between the bare semiconductor die and an integrated heat spreader (IHS) during internal chip packaging.
- **TIM 2:** Materials applied between the exterior of the packaged chip/heat spreader and the external cooling heat sink. [[1](https://prod-edam.honeywell.com/content/dam/honeywell-edam/pmt/oneam/en-us/electronic-materials/thermal-interface-materials/documents/hon-ess-adm-the-role-of-tims-in-modern-electronics.pdf)]

## MCL vs. TIM

在微通道蓋板（MCL）的架構中，我們依然非常需要 TIM，但我們只保留了「第一層」（TIM1），並徹底消除了「第二層」（TIM2）。 [1, 2, 3] 
要理解為什麼不能完全丟掉 TIM，必須從晶片層級的散熱路徑（Thermal Path）來看這場結構變革：
## 傳統液冷 vs. MCL 液冷的熱路徑對比

* 傳統液冷（Blackwell 之前）：
$$\text{晶片 (Die)} \xrightarrow{\mathbf{TIM1}} \text{傳統金屬蓋 (IHS)} \xrightarrow{\mathbf{TIM2}} \text{外部液冷板 (Cold Plate)}$$ 
* MCL 液冷（Vera Rubin 世代）：
$$\text{晶片 (Die)} \xrightarrow{\mathbf{TIM1}} \text{微通道蓋板 (MCL，內有水流)}$$ 
[1, 4, 5] 

MCL 的核心物理邏輯是把「傳統金屬蓋」與「外部液冷板」合二為一。因為液冷水道直接刻在金屬蓋內部，外部不再需要單獨鎖一塊冷板，因此原本夾在冷板與晶片蓋之間的 TIM2 被完全消除了。這拿掉了傳統散熱中最大的一道熱阻瓶頸。 [1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7] 
------------------------------
## 為什麼我們絕對無法拋棄 TIM1？
即使冷卻水流已經移到晶片的頭頂（MCL 內部），在「矽晶片表面」與「MCL 底面」之間，TIM1 依然是不可或缺的防線，原因有以下三個致命的物理限制： [4, 8] 
## 1. 微觀下的「空氣熱絕緣牆」
無論 [TSMC](https://semiwiki.com/semiconductor-manufacturers/362017-breaking-the-thermal-wall-tsmc-demonstrates-direct-to-silicon-liquid-cooling-on-cowos/) 把晶片表面拋光得再完美，或者 [Jentech (健策)](https://www.jentech.com.tw/zh/vapor-chamber-lid-2) 的金屬蓋加工多精密，在顯微鏡下，兩個固體表面接觸時依然佈滿了肉眼看不見的粗糙凹凸與坑洞。
如果不用 TIM1 去填滿，這些坑洞裡就會充滿空氣。空氣的導熱率極低（約 0.026 W/m⋅K），會形成一道巨大的熱絕緣牆，讓 1,000W+ 的 GPU 晶片瞬間因熱量無法排出而燒毀。
## 2. 先進封裝的「晶片翹曲 (Warpage)」
AI 晶片（如 CoWoS 封裝）是由多個邏輯晶片（Logic Die）和高頻寬記憶體（HBM）並排組成的。在晶片運作升溫時，由於矽、基板、PCB 的熱膨脹係數不同，整個封裝會產生微觀的翹曲（Warpage）與高度差。
TIM1（如銦片或流體狀導熱膏）具有物理延展性與彈性，能扮演緩衝墊，完美填補因晶片翹曲產生的動態縫隙。 [7, 9, 10] 
## 3. 保護脆弱的矽晶片
如果讓堅硬的銅製 MCL 直接硬碰硬壓在極其脆弱、薄如蟬翼的矽晶片（Silicon Die）上，在伺服器組裝加壓或震動時，晶片會直接被壓碎或產生微裂紋（Micro-cracks）。TIM1 提供了必要的機械保護介面。 [5] 
------------------------------
## 技術演進：MCL 語境下的 TIM1 材質升級
因為晶片功耗太高，傳統導熱係數僅有 3 ~ 8 W/m⋅K 的散熱膏（Grease）在 TIM1 已經不夠用了。在 [NVIDIA Vera Rubin](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/nvidia-vera-rubins-new-microchannel-liquid-cold-plate-mark-seery-btmhc) 及未來的 MCL 時代，TIM1 正在全面走向高階材料： [5] 

* 銦片（Indium Metal TIM）： 導熱係數高達約 80 W/m⋅K，利用固態金屬片的延展性，在高壓封裝時緊密填滿縫隙，目前是千瓦級晶片的主流。
* 液態金屬（Liquid Metal / 鎵銦合金）： 導熱係數更可超過 30 ~ 60 W/m⋅K，且處於液態，熱阻更低。但因為具備導電性且會腐蝕鋁/銅，需要晶圓廠做極其精密的邊緣密封工藝（Conformal Coating）。 [9, 11, 12] 

## 什麼時候連 TIM1 都可以不要？
只有當技術演進到前文提到的**「Direct-to-Silicon（直接晶圓級冷卻）」**時，TIM1 才會消失。也就是連 MCL（金屬蓋）都不要了，直接由 TSMC 透過晶圓鍵合（Fusion Bonding），把刻有水路的矽上蓋與晶片永久熔接在一起。但在 **MCL（蓋板液冷）**的商業架構下，TIM1 依然是必不可少的存在。 [1, 5, 13]

## MCL Challenges and Risks

The transition to Microchannel Lids (MCL) introduces severe manufacturing risks because it forces mechanical fluid engineering into microfluidic scales, requiring tolerances under $\pm\text{0.01 mm}$. A manufacturing defect in a standard cold plate ruins a replaceable $200 metal part, but a defect in an MCL instantly bricks a fully packaged multi-thousand-dollar AI chip. [1] 
The main manufacturability failure modes and risks for MCL are structured by their production stages: [2] 
## 1. Micro-Machining & Etching Defects
The internal channels of an MCL are incredibly narrow, typically between 50 to 150 micrometers wide. [1] 

* Micro-Burrs and Debris: When high-precision milling or chemical etching cuts these channels into the oxygen-free copper substrate, microscopic metal burrs or residue can remain. If not perfectly cleaned, these particles can break free under high flow rates and immediately cause microfluidic clogging, causing localized hotspots that fry the GPU. [3, 4, 5, 6] 
* Channel Geometry Variation: Even a slight deviation in the depth or width of the microchannels alters fluidic resistance. This imbalance causes unequal pressure drops across the chip, starving critical areas (like the HBM4e stacks or the logic die) of uniform water flow. [5, 7] 

## 2. Brazing, Welding, and Sealing Failure (The Leak Risk)
MCLs are typically two-piece structures—a micro-channeled base plate and a 3D manifold cap layer—that must be sealed together. [1, 8] 

* Vacuum Brazing Voids: Manufacturers like [Jentech](https://www.jentech.com.tw/vapor-chamber-lid-2) rely on high-vacuum brazing or laser welding to bond the layers. If micro-voids, bubbles, or uneven filler metal deposition occur during heating, the internal seal fails. [4] 
* Delayed Internal Rupture: This layer is subjected to continuous high-pressure liquid flow. Voids can act as stress-concentration points. Over time, the internal walls can rupture under pressure, mixing the input "cold water" directly with outgoing "hot water" before it can scrub the chip's surface. [1, 9, 10] 

## 3. Advanced Packaging Placement & Warpage Stresses
Unlike external cold plates bolted onto a computer, the MCL is glued, sealed, and assembled directly onto the substrate at the Advanced Packaging (OSAT) plant. [6] 

* CoWoS Multi-Die Height Differences: In a large interposer package (like Nvidia's Rubin Ultra modular architecture), the height of the GPU dies and the surrounding HBM4e dies may have tiny micrometer-level height variances. [11, 12] 
* Warpage-Induced TIM1 Delamination: Because the MCL is a stiff, monolithic block of copper, forcing it onto warped silicon substrates introduces immense mechanical pressure fatigue. During the reflow and thermal cycling stages, this mismatch can tear the TIM1 (Indium or Liquid Metal layer) away from the die, causing instant thermal failure. [13, 14, 15] 

## 4. Electroplating and Chemical Compatibility Risk
To protect the internal copper from interacting with water and glycol coolants, the microchannels require electroplating with protective layers of nickel or gold. [3, 14] 

* Plating Pinholes: Electroplating inside a 100-micron channel is highly complex. If the plating is uneven or contains microscopic pinholes, the aggressive liquid flow will cause microfluidic erosion-corrosion. Copper ions will flake off, clogging downstream microchannels and rapidly eating through the lid wall until the coolant breaks out into the electronics. [1, 5, 16, 17] 

------------------------------
## How the Supply Chain Solves This
To combat these failure modes, the manufacturing process now integrates semiconductor-grade inspection lines. Vendors must employ acoustic microscopy (C-SAM) and X-ray computed tomography (CT) to inspect 100% of the internal brazing layers for sub-surface voids, and enforce strict automated high-pressure helium leak testing before the MCL ever touches the packaging line. ## Manifold

在伺服器散熱和半導體封裝（如 Microchannel Lid, MCL）的語境中，Manifold（分流管 / 集水器 / 歧管） 的核心角色就是**「流量交通警察」**。

不論是在晶片內部（晶圓/上蓋層級）還是在伺服器外部（機櫃層級），Manifold 的唯一目的，就是將一條大水流均勻地拆分成無數條極細的微小水流，讓它們精準沖刷發熱點，隨後再將這些熱水安全地匯集起來帶走。

在 MCL 與先進散熱的架構中，Manifold 主要出現在以下兩個層級，其工程意義完全不同：

---

## 1. 晶片內部層級：封裝/上蓋內的微型分流（In-Lid Manifold）

當液冷技術演進到 MCL 時，冷卻水是直接進入晶片金屬蓋（Lid）內部的微米級溝槽（Microchannels）。此時，晶片上蓋的結構通常會被拆成兩層： [1]

- 下層（微通道層）： 直接接觸矽晶片，上面刻有成百上千條寬度僅有幾十微米（比頭髮還細）的微細溝槽。
- 上層（Manifold 分流層）： 這就是 In-Lid Manifold。因為微通道太細、阻力（壓降）極大，液體如果只從左端一路流到右端，流速會變慢且後段水溫會過高。Manifold 層利用複雜的 3D 立體管道結構（類似立交橋），把進來的冷水從「垂直方向」均勻地「灌入」各個微通道的中段或特定熱點（Hotspots），並在吸熱後將廢水向上抽離。

為什麼這需要晶圓廠（如 TSMC）和先進封裝廠（OSAT）深度參與？  
因為這個微型 Manifold 的加工精度要求達到了微米級（Micro-level），必須藉由半導體蝕刻或高精度微加工（如雷射、3D 金屬列印）來製作，且必須保證在極高的水壓下封裝絕對不漏水。

---

## 2. 伺服器/機櫃層級：巨觀分流管（Rack/Chassis Manifold）

如果您在新聞或供應鏈報告中看到「液冷機櫃導入 Manifold」，這通常指的是機櫃後方或伺服器內部的不鏽鋼或銅製主管線。 [2]

- 機櫃分流管（Rack Manifold）： 是一根直立在整座機櫃後方的金屬長管。一根負責將來自冷卻分配總成（CDU）的冷水，均勻分流到這座機櫃裡的 72 顆或 576 顆 GPU 節點中；另一根負責回收所有節點排出的熱水。
- 節點分流管（Chassis Manifold）： 在單一伺服器外殼內，將進來的水流拆分給左邊的 GPU、右邊的 GPU 以及 HBM 記憶體。 [3]

---

## MCL 語境下 Manifold 的三大工程挑戰

1. 壓降與流速平衡（Pressure Drop & Flow Uniformity）：  
    AI 晶片上的發熱極度不均勻（運算核心極熱、周邊記憶體較溫）。Manifold 的微通道設計必須做到「晶片哪裡熱，水流就往哪裡多灌」，這需要極度複雜的流體力學（CFD）模擬。
2. 極致的防腐蝕與防堵塞（Anti-clogging）：  
    因為 MCL 內部的微通道和 Manifold 孔徑極小，水質只要有一點點雜質、微粒或金屬剝落，管路就會立刻堵塞導致 GPU 燒毀。因此 Manifold 的材料選擇（通常是高純度銅或不鏽鋼）和塗層工藝要求極高。
3. 熱膨脹失配（CTE Mismatch）：  
    當 GPU 滿載時，矽晶片、微型 Manifold 上蓋和外部接頭會同時劇烈升溫。這三種不同材料的膨脹係數不同，如果 Manifold 結構設計不當，微小的變形就會導致密封圈失效，引發災難性的漏水。 [4]

如果您想深入了解，我們可以探討 TSMC 在 CoWoS 封裝中如何利用矽基板（Silicon Interposer）直接整合微流道 Manifold 的技術細節，或是了解台廠供應鏈（如雙鴻、高力、奇鋐）在機櫃級 Manifold 的競爭狀況。

  
## Silicon Interposer with Micro-Channel

是的，矽中介層（Silicon Interposer）絕對可以、而且在技術上已經成功整合了微通道（Micro-channels）！ [1]

在先進封裝（如 [TSMC](https://3dfabric.tsmc.com/chinese/dedicatedFoundry/technology/cowos.htm?ref=thestack.technology) CoWoS 或是 Intel EMIB）的語境下，將微流道直接整合進矽中介層（Embedded Microfluidic Cooling in Interposer）是目前半導體界解決 3D 晶片堆疊散熱瓶頸的終極技術之一。 [2, 3, 4, 5]

不過，「中介層微通道」 與前文提到的 「MCL（上蓋微通道）」 在半導體結構中的位置、冷卻邏輯以及工程挑戰完全不同。

---

## 一、 為什麼要在 Silicon Interposer 裡挖微通道？

在傳統的 2.5D/3D 封裝中，多個運算晶片（如邏輯 SoC）與 HBM 記憶體是排排站「貼」在矽中介層上。 [6]

- 傳統背部散熱（MCL 邏輯）： 散熱水流在晶片的「頭頂」（背面 silicon 或金屬 Lid）流過。
- 中介層散熱（Interposer 邏輯）： 散熱水流從晶片的「腳底」（晶片底部的 Interposer）穿過。 [1]

兩者的致命差異在於「3D 堆疊」： 當未來晶片走向 3D 縱向堆疊（例如 HBM 下方再疊運算晶片，或是晶片層層相疊），夾在中間層的晶片熱量根本無法傳導到頭頂的 MCL 上蓋。 此時，利用半導體蝕刻製程，直接在底部的 Silicon Interposer 內部挖出微流道和微型分流器（Manifold），讓冷卻液從晶片正下方直接帶走熱量，就成了唯一的解法。 [1, 7]

---

## 二、 兩大散熱技術的直接對比：MCL vs. Silicon Interposer 微通道

|比較項目 [1, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13]|MCL (Microchannel Lid / IMC-Si)|Embedded Interposer Microfluidics|
|---|---|---|
|位置|晶片的頂部/背面（Backside Cooling）|晶片的底部/中介層（Bottom/Under-die Cooling）|
|主要技術擁有者|[TSMC](https://semiwiki.com/semiconductor-manufacturers/362017-breaking-the-thermal-wall-tsmc-demonstrates-direct-to-silicon-liquid-cooling-on-cowos/) 與 專利金屬蓋廠（如 Jentech 健策）|TSMC / Intel / IMEC 等晶圓廠（純晶圓級製程）|
|主要製程工藝|在晶片背面製作微柱，再用膠或熔接結合有微通道的金屬/矽蓋。|使用 DRIE（深離子蝕刻） 直接在矽晶圓上挖通道，再進行晶圓鍵合（Wafer Bonding）。|
|冷卻對象|傳統的 2.5D 晶片（如 Nvidia Blackwell / Rubin，晶片頂部接觸得到水）。|複雜的 3D IC 堆疊、光電共同封裝（CPO）或晶片與中介層緊密結合的系統。|
|商業化進度|2025-2026 已進入市場實用階段，準備量產。|仍多處於研發（R&D）與高階利基實驗階段（如 CERN 的高能物理探測器）。|

---

## 三、 Silicon Interposer 整合微通道的「夢幻優勢」

1. 熱膨脹係數（CTE）完美匹配： 因為中介層本來就是矽（Silicon），微通道也是在矽上面挖，兩者受熱膨脹的比例完全一致，徹底解決了金屬上蓋與矽晶片因膨脹不同步導致接口撕裂漏水的宿疾。
2. 極致的超高密度布線（RDL）： 利用矽製程，可以在中介層正面做極細的電路布線（Redistribution Layer, RDL）給晶片導電，背面或中層挖水路散熱，做到電、水並存的極致異質整合。
3. 消除所有 TIM（熱介面材料）： 水流距離發熱的矽晶片微凸塊（Micro-bumps）只有幾十微米的距離，熱阻趨近於零。 [1, 8, 11, 14, 15, 16]

---

## 四、 為什麼當前的 AI 晶片（如 Rubin）不優先用 Interposer 散熱？

既然中介層微通道這麼完美，為什麼 Nvidia 傳出的 Feynman 或 Rubin 世代還是優先考慮 MCL（上蓋微通道）？因為 Interposer 液冷有以下幾個毀滅性的商業與工程代價：

1. 矽面積的極度浪費： 矽中介層（如 CoWoS-S）本身的晶圓成本非常高昂。要在裡面挖出巨大的流體管道、進出水口（Fluidic Vias），會嚴重壓縮到原本用來走電路訊號與 TSV（矽穿孔）的空間。
2. 流體機械應力與震動： 當高壓冷卻水在微觀的矽中介層內部流動時，產生的微小流體震動（Micro-cavitation）可能會直接破壞中介層上極其脆弱的 sub-micron（亞微米）銅線，導致晶片訊號斷裂。
3. 「水走一走，變導電媒介」的風險： 矽雖然是半導體，但水管如果萬一產生微觀滲漏，高壓冷卻水直接接觸到 2.5D 封裝內數萬根密集的微凸塊訊號線（Micro-bumps），晶片會瞬間短路燒毀。 [9, 14, 16, 17, 18, 19]

## 總結

技術上完全可行，學術界與晶圓廠（如 TSMC 的研發部門）已經有很多發表成果，能利用 DRIE 技術在中介層內完美刻出 Micro-channels 與 Manifold。 [8, 11]

然而在商務現實上，因為成本、電訊號干擾、以及製程良率的考量，當前主流依然會是把微通道做在晶片的「頭頂（MCL / 晶片背面 silicon 直接冷卻）」。中介層微通道散熱，將會保留給未來更高密度、連頭頂都沒空間散熱的 3D 超級晶片堆疊 時代。 [2, 7, 8, 12, 20]

## MCL vs. Immersion: The Battle of Water vs. Oil

To understand why Nvidia is choosing Microchannel Lids (MCL) over Immersion for its roadmap, you must look at the physics: Water is a superior coolant to oil. [1]

- Heat Capacity: Water holds ~2x more heat energy per volume than the dielectric fluids (oils) used in immersion.
- Viscosity: Water is thinner and flows faster.
- Result: For extreme "hot spots" (like the GB200 or Vera Rubin GPU die), high-velocity water in microchannels can scrub heat away from the silicon surface much faster than oil can, even if the oil submerges the whole board. [2, 3, 4, 5, 6]

## 1. Technology Comparison

|Feature [6, 7, 8, 9, 10]|Microchannel Lids (MCL) / Direct-to-Chip|Immersion Cooling (Single/Two-Phase)|
|---|---|---|
|Primary Mechanism|Precision: Water flows through microscopic channels etched directly into the chip's lid (or very close to it), targeting the GPU die.|Holistic: The entire server rack is submerged in a "bath" of non-conductive dielectric fluid (oil).|
|Cooling Target|Hot Spots Only: Cools the GPU/CPU/HBM. Other components (capacitors, VRMs) still need air or cold plates.|Everything: Cools 100% of the board, including memory, power delivery, and solders.|
|Heat Transfer|Extreme: Handles massive heat flux (>1,000 W/cm²) due to water's superior conductivity.|Moderate: Dielectric fluid is less conductive. Often struggles with extreme point-heat without "impingement jets."|
|Infrastructure|Rack-Friendly: Fits in standard vertical racks. Requires complex plumbing (manifolds) inside the rack.|Facility-Heavy: Requires horizontal "tanks" (like freezers), reinforced floors, and cranes to lift servers.|

## 2. Pros & Cons

Microchannel Lids (MCL) & Direct-to-Chip (Nvidia's Choice)

- Pros:
    
    - Highest Performance: Can handle the extreme heat density of future 1,000W+ chips where oil would "boil off" or saturate too locally.
    - Retrofit Friendly: Can be deployed in vertical racks in existing data centers (with added plumbing).
    - Maintenance: IT staff can still "hot swap" blades from the front (using dripless connectors) without needing a crane. [6, 11]
    
- Cons:
    
    - Leak Risk: Hundreds of connections per rack mean a higher statistical risk of a catastrophic water leak.
    - Partial Cooling: Doesn't cool the rest of the motherboard, often requiring fans or auxiliary cold plates for lesser components. [6, 12]
    

Immersion Cooling

- Pros:
    
    - Silent & Clean: No fans means near-silent operation and zero dust/corrosion on hardware.
    - Thermal Stability: The huge volume of fluid creates a massive "thermal flywheel," preventing temperature spikes.
    - Energy Efficiency: Can achieve near-perfect PUE (1.02–1.05) by eliminating all fans. [6, 11, 13, 14, 15]
    
- Cons:
    
    - The "Flux" Barrier: Dielectric fluid often cannot remove heat fast enough from a tiny, super-hot GPU die (Rubin/Blackwell) without complex "jet" nozzles.
    - Messy Ops: Servicing a broken stick of RAM involves pulling a dripping server out of a tank of oil.
    - Cost: High initial cost for fluids (especially two-phase fluids like 3M Novec, which face environmental regulation issues). [6, 15, 16, 17, 18]
    

---

## When will Immersive Cooling be used? (Or Never?)

Short Answer: It will likely remain a niche solution, not the standard. [19, 20, 21]

Nvidia's roadmap for Vera Rubin (2026) and Rosa Feynman (2028) explicitly favors Warm Water Direct-to-Chip, not immersion. [6, 22]

1. The "Warranty Wall": As of now, Nvidia does not generally offer lifespan certification or standard warranties for chips used in immersion tanks. Modifying a GPU (removing fans/heatsinks to dunk it) typically voids the warranty unless bought through a specialized partner (like GIGABYTE or Supermicro) who assumes that risk. [6, 23, 24, 25, 26]
2. The Physics Limit: As chips get smaller and hotter (higher density), they hit the "Heat Flux Limit." Oil is simply too thick and holds too little heat to cool a >1000W chip effectively without expensive engineering (like boiling it in Two-Phase immersion, which has environmental concerns). [27, 28, 29, 30, 31]
3. The "Never" Scenario: It will likely never be the default for the mass market (hyperscalers like AWS/Azure) for top-tier training chips because the infrastructure change (tanks vs racks) is too radical. [6, 32]

Exception: You will see immersion used in Greenfield Projects (brand new data centers built from scratch) in hot climates (like the Middle East) where using air cooling is impossible and water scarcity makes evaporation towers viable. In these specific cases, partners will build custom "tank farms" for Nvidia chips. [6, 33, 34, 35]

為了讓您更易於掃描，以下是 MCL 與您提及的各關鍵技術的相對幾何位置與功能互動：

* 相對於 HBM4e Stack 與 GPU Die：在它們的「正上方」
* 幾何位置： MCL 的**底面（Base）**透過 TIM1 介面，直接硬碰硬壓在 GPU 晶粒和 HBM4e 堆疊的裸晶背面（Silicon Backside）。
   * 功能關係： MCL 的微流道（Microchannels）就刻在直接對準 GPU 核心與 HBM4e 的正上方，冷卻水流在這些發熱源頭頂上方僅幾十微米處流過，以超短路徑強行將熱量吸走。
* 相對於 CoWoS 封裝技術：它是 CoWoS 的「外部封裝外殼」
* 幾何位置： CoWoS 泛指底部的中介層、GPU 晶粒與 HBM 堆疊異質整合在一起的完整晶片。MCL 則是封裝完成後，用來罩住整個 CoWoS 系統的保護蓋兼散熱器。
   * 功能關係： MCL 的尺寸必須完美覆蓋整顆 CoWoS 晶片的面積，通常由 OSAT（先進封裝廠）在 CoWoS 製程的最後一道關卡，將 MCL 黏合（Lid Bonding）上去。 [3] 
* 相對於 Interposer（中介層）：隔著晶片核心，在「最上層」
* 幾何位置： 中介層在晶片的**「腳底」（下方），MCL 在晶片的「頭頂」**（上方）。兩者將 GPU 晶粒與 HBM 堆疊像夾心餅乾一樣夾在中間。
   * 功能關係： 中介層在下方負責「電訊號傳輸」，MCL 在上方負責「液冷散熱」，兩者各司其職，實現了 Rubin Ultra 電、水完全分離的異質整合設計。

## MCL Mechanic and Thermal Risk
是的，MCL（微通道蓋板）在大幅提升散熱效率的同時，也對 HBM、中介層（Interposer）以及整個封裝（Package）帶來了極高且連帶的「機械應力」與「動態熱應力」風險。
在單顆晶片功耗高達 3,000W–3,600W 的 Rubin Ultra 世代，MCL 的設計就像在極其脆弱的玻璃骨架（矽晶圓）上安裝一個高壓液壓鋼盔，其帶來的核心風險可以分為以下三大維度：
------------------------------
## 1. 對 HBM 堆疊（HBM Stack）的風險：非對稱壓應力與微凸塊裂化
HBM4/4e 內部是由 12 層或 16 層極薄的記憶體晶粒（Die）透過 TSV（矽穿孔）與微凸塊（Micro-bumps）縱向垂直堆疊而成，結構非常脆弱。 [1] 

* 「階梯式」非對稱壓應力（Asymmetric Stress）：
GPU 邏輯晶粒與 HBM 堆疊在晶圓減薄製程後的最終高度通常存在微米級的高度差（Height Variation）。MCL 的底面必須加工出對應的「階梯面」來同時貼合兩者。如果加工精度不夠（誤差大於 ± 5μ m），MCL 蓋下去時會對 HBM 造成嚴重的局部偏心壓應力，直接導致 HBM 內部脆弱的 3D 堆疊層結構變形（Die Warpage）。 [2] 
* 晶粒微凸塊斷裂（Micro-bump Cracking）：
MCL 內部充滿了高壓流動的冷卻水（甚至有垂直噴淋設計）。水流在微通道內產生的**微觀流體震動（Hydraulic Vibration）**會穿過 TIM1 直接傳導到 HBM 頂部。這種高頻微震動在經過多次熱循環後，極易導致 HBM 底部成千上萬個直徑僅約 10-20 微米的微凸塊產生疲勞裂紋（Fatigue Cracking），引發記憶體訊號中斷。

------------------------------
## 2. 對中介層（Interposer / CoWoS）的風險：熱膨脹失配與剪切應力
矽中介層（Silicon Interposer）是承載 GPU 與 HBM 的玻璃狀大平台，它非常硬且脆，最怕拉扯和扭曲。

* 巨大的 CTE 熱膨脹失配（CTE Mismatch）：
MCL 的本體通常是金屬銅（熱膨脹係數 CTE ≈ 17），而下方的晶片和中介層都是矽（CTE ≈ 2.6）。當晶片在 0W 到 3600W 之間劇烈動態變溫時，頂部的銅蓋會比底部的矽中介層膨脹和收縮得快得多。
* 邊緣剪切應力（Shear Stress）：
這種頂部拉、底部不動的物理現象，會在中介層邊緣產生巨大的橫向剪切應力。這股應力會向下傳遞，導致中介層本身的電路布線層（RDL）剝離，或是直接讓中介層產生微觀的裂應力（Crack propagation），導致整顆 CoWoS 晶片報廢。

------------------------------
## 3. 對整體封裝（Package Substrate）的風險：香蕉化（嚴重翹曲）
最下層的有機基板（Substrate，如 ABF 基板）非常大且具有彈性，是整個封裝中最容易變形的元件。

* 動態翹曲（Dynamic Warpage / 香蕉化）：
為了固定 MCL，OSAT 廠會在 MCL 的四周邊緣塗上強力的結構膠（Sealant）將其黏死在基板上。當整顆晶片加溫運作時，由於上述的金屬（MCL）、矽（Die/Interposer）與有機物（Substrate）三者的熱膨脹完全不對稱，整顆晶片會像烤香蕉一樣向上或向下彎曲（Warpage 可達數百微米）。
* BGA 錫球脫焊：
當封裝基板被 MCL 邊緣的拉力強行扯彎時，基板底部用來連接系統主機板（PCB）的數千顆 BGA 錫球（Ball Grid Array） 就會因受力不均而被拉斷或擠壓短路。這會導致伺服器運行到一半時突然偵測不到 GPU 模組。

------------------------------
## MCL 語境下的供應鏈解法與防線
為了解決這些可怕的機械與熱應力風險，Nvidia 與 TSMC、Jentech（健策）等供應鏈在 Rubin 世代導入了新的材料工程：

   1. 新型低 CTE 蓋板材料： 放棄純銅，轉向開發**銅-鑽石複合材料（Copper-Diamond Composite）或矽碳化鋁（AlSiC）**來製作 MCL，將蓋板的 CTE 從 17 降到 6-8 左右，主動貼近矽的特性。
   2. 高彈性/自我修復 TIM1： 捨棄剛性的硬質銦片，在 Rubin Ultra 的特定過渡熱區改用具備流動彈性的高分子液態金屬（Liquid Metal with containment gaskets），吸收 GPU 與 HBM 之間的高度差和微震動。
   3. 加強肋（Stiffener Ring）設計： 在基板周圍埋入高剛性的不鏽鋼加強環，強行鎖死基板的翹曲幅度，不讓 MCL 的金屬拉力破壞底部的 BGA 錫球。
